Money Leak Finder

Your data never leaves your device.

Find the subscriptions, repeat charges, double payments and price rises hiding in your bank or card statement.

Drop your statement here CSV or Excel (.xlsx). You can add several files at once.

Getting your statement file

  1. Log in to your bank or card account on a computer. Phone apps often can't export.
  2. Open your transactions and look for Export, Download or Statements.
  3. Choose CSV or Excel, and pick the longest period you can. Six to twelve months finds the most.

PDF statements won't work. If your bank only offers PDF, look for "Download transactions" instead of "Statements".

What it looks for

Recurring chargesSame company, similar amount, on a regular schedule.
Forgotten subscriptionsSmall monthly charges that are easy to miss.
Double paymentsThe same amount charged twice by the same company within 3 days.
Price risesSubscriptions that quietly started charging more.

Everything happens in this browser tab. Nothing is uploaded, stored or sent anywhere. The page cannot connect to the internet, and it works offline. Close the tab and it's all gone.
